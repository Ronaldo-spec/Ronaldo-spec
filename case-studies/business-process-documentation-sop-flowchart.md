# Business Process Documentation, SOP & Flowchart — Sanitized Case Study

> This case study is sanitized. Company-specific procedures, document codes, internal rules, names, and sensitive operational details are generalized.

## Summary

This case study demonstrates my experience in documenting business processes through workflow analysis, SOP structure, flowchart preparation, and user-friendly process explanation.

The work focused on making operational processes clearer, more standardized, and easier to transfer between users or team members.

## Business Context

Business processes often involve many roles, documents, approval points, system steps, and follow-up actions. Without clear documentation, users may interpret the same process differently.

This can create inconsistent execution, unclear responsibilities, repeated questions, and difficulty when onboarding new users or changing system features.

## Problem

The process needed clearer documentation so users and stakeholders could understand:

- Who performs each step.
- What document or system action is required.
- What condition triggers the next step.
- What exception or decision point must be handled.
- How the process connects to ERP/application workflow.

## My Role

I supported the documentation by:

- Reviewing the current process flow.
- Separating process stages into logical sections.
- Identifying decision points and role responsibilities.
- Structuring SOP content with consistent numbering and headings.
- Preparing flowchart-friendly process logic.
- Aligning documentation with the actual operational workflow.

## Approach

### 1. Process understanding

I started by reviewing the process objective, involved roles, required documents, system actions, and business rules.

### 2. Workflow breakdown

The process was broken down into structured stages so each step could be understood in order.

### 3. Decision point identification

I identified conditions, exceptions, approvals, and status changes that affected the next process step.

### 4. SOP structuring

I organized the documentation using clear headings, numbering, and consistent phrasing to make it easier to read and maintain.

### 5. Flowchart preparation

I converted the process into flowchart-ready logic so it could be visualized using tools such as draw.io, Lucidchart, or other diagramming tools.

## Deliverables

- Process flow summary.
- SOP-style documentation.
- Flowchart-ready process logic.
- Role and responsibility mapping.
- Decision point notes.
- Documentation revision suggestions.

## Tools & Skills

- Technical writing
- User communication

## Result

The documentation made the process easier to understand, review, revise, and transfer to other users. It also helped connect business operations with ERP/application workflow more clearly.
